Liteclerk Corporation was founded with a simple goal: to help businesses eliminate operational complexity through reliable and easy-to-use software solutions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower small businesses by providing a robust, easy-to-use platform that simplifies administrative and operational tasks. We are dedicated to delivering innovative solutions that help business owners save time, reduce costs, and enhance their overall efficiency.

Through our commitment to exceptional service and continuous improvement, we strive to:

  1. Simplify Business Management: Offer intuitive tools that make managing finances, inventory, sales, and customer relationships effortless.
  2. Support Growth and Innovation: Provide resources that enable small businesses to scale and innovate without being bogged down by administrative burdens.
  3. Enhance Accessibility: Ensure our platform is accessible and affordable for all small businesses, regardless of their size or industry.
  4. Foster a Community: Build a supportive community of small business owners who can share insights, advice, and encouragement.

By aligning our efforts with the needs of small business owners, we aim to be the trusted partner they rely on for operational success and sustainable growth.

Our Vision

Our vision is to revolutionize the way small and large businesses manage their operations by providing an intuitive, efficient, and comprehensive digital platform. We aspire to empower entrepreneurs with the tools they need to streamline their administrative tasks, enabling them to focus on growth, innovation, and customer satisfaction.

We envision a future where every business, regardless of its size or industry, can access advanced business management solutions that are typically reserved for multinational enterprises. By leveraging cutting-edge technology and user-centric design, we aim to eliminate the barriers that hinder productivity and efficiency, making business management simple, accessible, and affordable.

Our commitment is to continuously innovate and improve our platform, ensuring it adapts to the evolving needs of the modern business landscape. Through collaboration with our users, ongoing development, and a dedication to excellence, we strive to be the go-to resource for small businesses seeking to thrive in a competitive market.

Our Background

Liteclerk Corporation is a 100% Filipino-owned software company. We specialize in developing software for POS (Point-Of-Sale), inventory management, and financial accounting.

Starting in 2008 with a small office in Cebu, we have expanded our reach nationwide. Our journey is defined by constant adaptation to the changing technological landscape, from desktop applications to full cloud-native solutions.
